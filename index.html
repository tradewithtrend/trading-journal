Why 100 Consecutive Trades?

In trading psychology, a sample size of fewer than 100 trades is statistically insignificant. A random win streak can make bad habits feel good, while a normal losing streak can destroy confidence. Your goal in Phase 1 is not to make \$1,000,000; it is to execute 100 disciplined, rule-based trades without revenge trading or moving stop losses.
