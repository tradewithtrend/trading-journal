The Mark Douglas 100-Trade Framework

Why the First 100 Trades Define Your Edge

In trading psychology, a sample size of fewer than 50-100 trades is statistically insignificant. A lucky streak can make a losing strategy look brilliant, while a normal 5-loss drawdown can cause you to abandon a winning system. Your sole mission in Phase 1 is to execute 100 trades with identical risk and strict discipline, logging every single one into Google Drive.

The 3 Golden Rules of This 100-Trade Journal

1. Fixed Risk Per Trade

Never risk more than 1% to 2% of total capital on any single trade during the 100-trade evaluation phase.

2. Journal Before Leaving Screen

Log every trade immediately upon closing. Waiting hours distorts emotional recall and lessons learned.

3. The Stop Loss is Holy

Never widen or remove a Stop Loss mid-trade. An initial stop loss hit is disciplined execution, not a failure.
